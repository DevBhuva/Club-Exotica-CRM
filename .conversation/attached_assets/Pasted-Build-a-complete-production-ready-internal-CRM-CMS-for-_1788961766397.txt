Build a complete production-ready internal CRM/CMS for a travel membership company.

COMPANY:

CLUB EXOTICA
Luxury Vacations Pvt. Ltd.

The system is an INTERNAL CUSTOMER, MEMBERSHIP, PAYMENT AND HOLIDAY REDEMPTION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM.

The system will initially be used by approximately 15 staff users simultaneously.

The application must be self-hostable on a Hostinger KVM VPS.

SUPER_ADMIN
